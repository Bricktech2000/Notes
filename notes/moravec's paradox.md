# Moravec's Paradox

**definition** _Moravec's paradox_ is the counterintuitive observation that abstract reasoning is trivial to machines but challenging to humans while motor and perception skills are trivial to humans but challenging to machines

> **note** there's a reinterpretation floating around that goes something like _Moravec's paradox is the counterintuitive observation that skills trivial to humans are challenging to machines while skills challenging to humans are trivial to machines_. but that's a silly thing to say; skills trivial or challenging to both humans and machines we unwittingly overlook because there is no [[cognitive dissonance]] to be resolved
