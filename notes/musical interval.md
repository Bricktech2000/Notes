# Musical Interval

**see** [[consonance]]

**definition** a _musical interval_ is a ratio of two [[frequency]]es

> **examples** _[[frequency]] ratios --- with names_
>
> - $\frac 1 1$ --- _unison_
> - $\frac 2 1$ --- _octave_
> - $\frac 5 3$ --- _major sixth_
> - $\frac 3 2$ --- _perfect fifth_
> - $\frac 4 3$ --- _perfect fourth_
> - $\frac 5 4$ --- _major third_
> - $\frac 6 5$ --- _minor third_
>
