# Eventual Control

_in the beginning you want results but in the end you want control_

**see** [[durability]], [[invent]], [[discover]]

> **resource** Eskil Steenberg: careful complaining about complexity when you just want results --- <https://youtu.be/443UNeGrFoM?t=149>

languages like [[java]] and [[go]] can feel like toys. it could be because they don't provide [[eventual control]]

things that provide control tend to be inconvenient when you just want results. but they're only inconvenient for so long, because over time [[procedural memory]] and chunking take the burden off your conscious mind

> **example** I disable sleep timeouts on all my devices. initially, having to remember to put them to sleep increases [[cognitive load]], but over time your brain does its thing and eventually it becomes automatic. it's like your devices can read your mind, because they're always awake when you need them awake and asleep when you need them asleep
