# Umwelt

--- Wikipedia

--- <https://youtu.be/4c1lqFXHvqI>

**definition** an organism's _umwelt_ is its model of the world, the slice of its ecosystem it can pick up on

> **examples** --- <https://youtu.be/4c1lqFXHvqI?t=141>
>
> - a tic's [[umwelt]] consists of [[temperature]] and buteric acid
> - a black ghost knifefish's [[umwelt]] consists of electrical fields
> - an ecolocating bat's [[umwelt]] consists of air compression waves

we are firmly settled in our [[umwelt]]. our perception of the world only feels objective because it's all we've ever known. say, we don't miss the world of smells dogs live in because we've never experienced it --- <https://youtu.be/4c1lqFXHvqI?t=198>. the truth is, our [[umwelt]] is surprisingly thin
